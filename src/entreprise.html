History

Our company was created from a student project and our professional experiences. We had all made the same observation during these different experiences: many companies lose efficiency due to inadequate management of their resources. From this analysis was born Strong Solutions, which aims to reinvent the consulting sector by guiding its clients through their transition period, without requiring significant financial contribution.

Why choose us?

At Strong Solutions, we are your preferred partner for optimizing your production in the industry. Our mission is to simplify your manufacturing processes, eliminate waste and improve your overall efficiency. Thanks to our Lean management expertise and our personalized approach, we help you achieve operational excellence.

Our innovative economic model is based on a win-win collaboration. We implement our solutions and we are compensated based on the real savings you will achieve thanks to our optimizations. Your success is our own success.

CSR Commitment

As a human-sized company, we strive to reduce our environmental impact by minimizing our resource needs. We also care about supporting our team members, so they can flourish professionally and personally.

Furthermore, our actions with our clients are consistent with our environmental commitments through the philosophy of lean management, which aims to eliminate waste of resources.
